The Postpartum Freezer Prep Checklist

Thirteen sessions. Six weeks. A freezer full of meals before baby arrives.
Rest and Rise · halfpintmama.com

This is the whole plan from Rest and Rise on one page. Follow the sessions in order — several recipes feed into later ones, so the sequence matters. Check off each session as you finish it, then flip the page and track what lands in the freezer.

Week 30 — Setup week (no baking yet)

Feed your starter daily for 3–4 days so it is strong and active
Take stock of equipment: Dutch oven, loaf pans, kitchen scale, parchment, 3-inch cutter, sandwich sealer — order what’s missing
Big pantry run + bulk freezer bags, disposable trays, masking tape, permanent marker
Optional head start: a batch (or double batch) of Instant Pot Chicken Bone Broth

Week 31 — Artisan loaves + first dinners

Session 1: Mix four artisan loaf doughs; during bulk ferment, assemble the Honey Garlic Chicken and Weeknight Butter Chicken freezer bags. Doughs shaped and into the fridge overnight.
Session 2: Bake all four loaves; between bakes, assemble the three Freezer Pork Shoulder bags and The House Chili.

Week 32 — English muffins + puff pastry

Session 3: Mix English muffin dough and a double batch of puff pastry (both cold-ferment overnight). Afternoon: Mini Sourdough Discard Pizzas.
Session 4: The big one — give it a full day. Griddle the English muffins, laminate the pastry, then make the Bacon Egg and Cheese Muffin Sandwiches, Italian Mini Quiches, and Puff Pastry Pinwheels.

Week 33 — Sweet bread, bars, broth

Session 5: Cinnamon Raisin Bread (bulk ferment by day, bake in the evening) + Apricot Cranberry Granola Bars while it rises.
Session 6: Start the Instant Pot Bone Broth first thing; mix and bake one Sourdough Sandwich Bread loaf for next week’s meatballs. Cool it completely.

Week 34 — Meatballs + tortillas

Session 7: Bake the sourdough-soaked meatballs, then build three recipes from them: Nesting Ziti (crumbled half), Aloha Meatballs bag (whole half), and the Khao Tom bag with session 6’s broth.
Session 8: Ferment tortilla + naan doughs in the morning; afternoon: cook tortillas, assemble Loaded Breakfast Tacos and Enchiladas That Show Up for You, cook and freeze the naan.

Week 35 — Casseroles + a treat

Session 9: Two rotisserie chickens, one shredding job: Creamy Confetti Chicken Casserole + Barbecue Chicken and Sweet Potato Casserole.
Session 10: Make the puff pastry reserved for session 13, then the Better Than the Box Beef and Pasta Bake, with Lactation Banana Bread in the oven while it cools.

Week 36 — The final stretch

Session 11: Sandwich bread bulk-ferments all afternoon while you mix cookie dough to chill, set the Chocolate Bark, and cook the Freezer Waffles. Bake the bread in the evening; cool overnight, unsliced.
Session 12: Flash-freeze cookie dough portions; bake the muffins; roll Energy Balls and Truffles; make Little Sealed Sandwiches and turn every trimmed crust into the Overnight French Toast Bake (fridge overnight).
Session 13: Bake the French Toast Bake; assemble the One-Handed Breakfast Pockets with session 10’s pastry. Final freezer walkthrough — you’re done.
Label everything: what it is, the date, and how to reheat it — written so anyone in the house can read the note and turn on the oven. Printable labels for every recipe live at halfpintmama.com/cookbook-resources.
