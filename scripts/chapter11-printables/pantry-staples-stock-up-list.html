Pantry Staples Stock-Up List

These are the ingredients that show up again and again throughout this book. Keep them stocked throughout your prep window and your weekly grocery run only needs to cover the fresh items for that session. You do not need to buy everything at once. Build this list gradually over the weeks leading up to your prep sessions and check things off as you go.
